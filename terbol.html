ABOUT TERBOL STATION

Small Ruminant Breeding & Research

Terbol Station is one of the LARI research stations located in Mid Bekaa.

The station specializes in breeding and selection of Awasi sheep and Shami goats.

Research also focuses on feeding and management of small ruminants, including the use of agro-industrial by-products in ruminant diets.

Evaluation of ruminant feeds and their nutritive value is also one of the station's tasks.

Terbol Station combines breeding, nutrition research, herd management, farmer support and practical training.

RESEARCH AREAS

Nutrition & Herd Management

Research focuses on improving feeding, management and resource use in small ruminants.

01

Small Ruminant Feeding

Research on suitable feeding systems for sheep and goats.

02

Herd Management

Studies related to effective management of small-ruminant herds.

03

Agro-Industrial By-Products

Evaluation of agro-industrial by-products for possible use in ruminant diets.

04

Feed Evaluation

Evaluation of feeds used for ruminant production.

05

Nutritive Value

Assessment of the nutritive value of ruminant feeds.

FARMER SERVICES

Supporting Sheep & Goat Farmers

Terbol Station provides improved breeding animals and technical advice to livestock farmers.

Improved Awasi Rams

Improved Awasi rams are sold yearly to interested farmers around Lebanon.

Improved Shami Bucks

Improved Shami bucks are also sold yearly to interested farmers.

Nutrition Advice

Local herders receive technical advice on flock nutrition.

Herd Management

Advice is provided on practical sheep and goat management.

Vaccination

Farmers are advised on vaccination of their sheep and goat herds.

TRAINING & COOPERATION

Research Partnerships & Practical Training

Terbol Station receives students and researchers for practical training and research in small-ruminant science.

01

Universities

Students and researchers from Lebanese universities are hosted at the station.

02

Research Institutes

Researchers from institutes in Lebanon can conduct small-ruminant research.

03

Agricultural Technical Schools

The station also receives trainees from agricultural technical schools.

04

Bilateral Agreements

As part of LARI, Terbol Station participates in bilateral agreements with other organizations.
