Plant Breeding

Supporting cereal improvement through plant breeding, wheat-rust epidemiology, variety evaluation and international research collaboration.

Plant Breeding Cereals Rust Research International Cooperation
PLANT BREEDING DEPARTMENT

Cereal Breeding & Rust Research

The Plant Breeding Department has wide expertise in rust epidemiology and variety release.

The department has collaborated with several international research centers in the cereal field.

Combining plant breeding, cereal research, rust epidemiology and international cooperation to support improved crop varieties.
RESEARCH EXPERTISE

Main Areas of Expertise

The department works mainly in cereal breeding, wheat-rust epidemiology, resistance evaluation and variety development.

01

Rust Epidemiology

Research on the epidemiology and pathogenic variability of cereal rusts.

Wheat Rust Research includes stripe or yellow rust caused by Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici.
02

Variety Release

Evaluation and release of improved crop varieties adapted to agricultural needs.

Improved Varieties Plant breeding supports the development and evaluation of improved cereal varieties.
03

Disease Resistance

Studying genetic resistance to important wheat rust diseases.

Resistance Diversity Research includes elite lines, commercial varieties and landraces from Lebanon and Syria.
04

Temperature & Pathogen Aggressiveness

Studying the effect of temperature on components of wheat-rust aggressiveness.

Disease Development Research considers infection efficiency, latency period and pathogen aggressiveness.
05

Pathotype Diversity

Studying diversity within wheat yellow-rust pathogen populations.

Lebanon & Syria Research has investigated Puccinia striiformis populations affecting wheat in Lebanon and Syria.
06

International Collaboration

Collaborating with leading international cereal research centers.

Research Centers Collaboration includes ICARDA, CIMMYT and BGRI.
INTERNATIONAL COLLABORATION

Global Cereal Research Partnerships

The Plant Breeding Department has collaborated with several major international institutions working on cereals and wheat rust.

ICARDA

International collaboration in cereal research and crop improvement.

CIMMYT

Cooperation in wheat, cereals and crop-improvement research.

BGRI

Collaboration related to wheat rust surveillance, resistance and research.

Research Network

Connecting LARI research with international cereal breeding expertise.

WHEAT RUST RESEARCH

Understanding Wheat Yellow Rust

A major research focus is the study of Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici and its interaction with wheat varieties.

01

Pathogen Variability

Monitoring variation in wheat-rust pathogen populations.

02

Temperature Effect

Evaluating how temperature affects infection efficiency and latency period.

03

Resistance Genes

Studying resistance diversity in wheat cultivars, landraces and elite genotypes.

04

Regional Surveillance

Research has examined yellow-rust populations in Lebanon and Syria.
