RANGELAND & FEED LABORATORY

Feed Quality & Animal Nutrition

The Rangeland and Feed Laboratory was established in 2003.

The laboratory serves forage-producing farmers, farm owners, feed mills, researchers, universities, students and departments of the Ministry of Agriculture.

It also supports veterinary quarantine, agricultural quarantine and the control of locally produced and imported feed.

Supporting feed quality, livestock nutrition, agricultural research and feed-control activities.
OUR ACTIVITIES

Laboratory & Research Activities

The laboratory combines nutritional analysis, education, research, monitoring and public communication activities.

01

Feed Nutritional Values

Determination of nutritional values for different types of animal feed and forage materials.

Materials Analyzed Fodder, concentrate, raw materials and rangeland components.
02

Educational Role

Providing practical education, training and awareness related to feed and animal nutrition.

Training Activities Training Lebanese University students and private university students, vocational students and organizing feed seminars for farmers.
03

Research Role

Conducting research aimed at improving feed quality and developing sustainable feeding solutions.

Research Areas Improving feed quality provided to animals, finding alternative feed adapted to climate change and fodder production using hydroponic systems.
04

Observer Role

Monitoring and supporting feed-control activities related to movement, import and export.

Monitoring Areas Border ports and fodder exports.
05

Communication & Public Relations

Supporting communication between the laboratory, farmers, institutions and agricultural stakeholders.

Outreach Promoting awareness, information exchange and cooperation related to animal feed and nutrition.
06

Hydroponic Fodder Production

Researching fodder production using hydroponic systems.

Sustainable Production Supporting alternative forage production methods adapted to changing environmental conditions.
LABORATORY SERVICES

Laboratory Tests

The laboratory performs analytical tests to determine the nutritional composition and quality of feed materials.

Dry Matter

Determination of dry matter content in feed samples.

Protein

Analysis of protein content in feed and forage materials.

Gross Energy

Measurement of the gross energy value of feed.

Crude Fat

Determination of crude fat content in samples.

Fiber

Analysis of fiber components, including NDF, ADF and ADL.

Urease Activity

Laboratory measurement of urease activity.

Ash

Determination of total mineral residue in feed.

pH

Measurement of acidity and alkalinity levels.

Minerals

Analysis of calcium, magnesium, phosphorus and other minerals.
