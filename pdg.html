Leadership

Dr. Jad Chaaya

Board of Directors and General Manager of LARI

Dr. Jad Chaaya leads the Lebanese Agricultural Research Institute and supports its role in agricultural research, innovation and the development of Lebanon's agricultural sector.

Through LARI's research stations, laboratories and scientific teams, the Institute works to strengthen agricultural knowledge and provide solutions that respond to the needs of farmers and the agricultural community.
