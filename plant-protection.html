PLANT PROTECTION DEPARTMENT

Protecting Crops & Plant Health

The Plant Protection Department focuses on the detection and identification of viruses, phytoplasmas, bacteria and fungi that infect strategic fruit crops and vegetables in Lebanon.

The department also develops improved management strategies for these diseases in order to reduce agricultural crop losses and protect plant health.

Combining field inspection, laboratory diagnostics and disease management to protect Lebanon's agricultural production.
OUR ACTIVITIES

Plant Protection Activities

Research activities cover crop health surveys, disease identification, certification, quarantine diseases, pest management and climate-related plant health risks.

01

Phytosanitary Evaluation

Evaluation of the sanitary status of major strategic crops and fruit trees throughout Lebanon.

Crops & Fruit Trees Potato, wheat, tomato, pepper, bean, sugar beet, cucurbit crops, olives, stone fruits, pome fruits, grapevine and citrus.
02

Disease Diagnosis

Identification and characterization of viral and phytoplasma diseases and their causal agents.

Diagnostic Methods Biological assays, serological assays, electron microscopy, ELISA, DTBIA, PCR, RFLP, molecular hybridization, dsRNA and real-time LAMP.
03

Bacterial & Fungal Diseases

Identification of bacterial and fungal plant diseases using modern laboratory diagnostic procedures.

Laboratory Techniques Isolation techniques, serological assays, molecular methods and pathogenicity tests.
04

Plant Certification

Supporting certification activities for healthy and reliable agricultural propagation material.

Certification Activities include the production of certified plant propagation material and plant-health assessment.
05

Emerging & Quarantine Diseases

Monitoring emerging and quarantine diseases threatening Mediterranean fruit and vegetable crops.

Important Threats Potato brown rot and ring rot, fire blight, Plum pox virus, phytoplasma diseases and Xylella fastidiosa.
06

Integrated Pest Management

Supporting Integrated Pest Management approaches for sustainable control of agricultural pests and diseases.

IPM Developing management approaches designed to reduce agricultural damage while supporting sustainable crop production.
07

Climate Change & Plant Health

Studying the potential impact of climate change on agricultural pests, diseases and plant-health risks.

Research Focus Understanding how changing environmental conditions may influence disease development and agricultural pest populations.
08

International Collaboration

Disease epidemiology and characterization are conducted in collaboration with European research institutes and universities.

Collaboration International cooperation supports advanced diagnostics, epidemiological research and characterization of important plant diseases.
RESEARCH FOCUS

Protecting Lebanon's Agricultural Production

Plant protection research provides the scientific information needed to detect plant diseases early, reduce agricultural losses and support healthier crop production.

Early Detection

Identifying plant pathogens through advanced laboratory diagnostic methods.

Disease Prevention

Supporting strategies to limit the spread and impact of plant diseases.

Certified Material

Contributing to the production of healthy certified plant propagation material.

Sustainable Protection

Supporting integrated and sustainable approaches to crop protection.
