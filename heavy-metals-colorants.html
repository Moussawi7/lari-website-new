ABOUT THE LABORATORY

Heavy Metals, Micronutrients & Dyes Analysis

The Laboratory of Heavy Metals and Dyes at LARI Fanar Station analyzes micronutrients in various types of food, fish and water.

Heavy metals are analyzed using Zeeman-Thermo Atomic Absorption Spectrometry with furnace technology.

Mercury and methylmercury analysis is performed using a Direct Mercury Analyzer, DMA 80.

Dyes in different foods and beverages are controlled using thin-layer chromatography, Automatic Development Chamber and TLC Viewer technologies.

In fish samples, malachite green and crystal violet are examined using the ELISA method.

The laboratory combines routine analytical work with scientific research and cooperation with LARI departments, stations, Lebanese universities and international partners.
ANALYTICAL AREAS

Main Laboratory Activities

The laboratory performs specialized testing for micronutrients, metals, mercury, colorants and selected residues.

01

Micronutrient Analysis

Analysis of micronutrients in different food products, fish and water samples.

Sample Types Food, fish and water.
02

Heavy Metals Analysis

Detection and quantification of metallic trace elements using atomic absorption spectrometry.

Technology Zeeman-Thermo Atomic Absorption Spectrometry Furnace.
03

Mercury & Methylmercury

Specialized analysis of mercury and methylmercury in samples.

Instrument Direct Mercury Analyzer DMA 80.
04

Food & Beverage Dyes

Monitoring dyes in different types of foods and beverages.

Technology Thin-layer chromatography, Automatic Development Chamber and TLC Viewer.
05

Fish Contaminant Testing

Examination of malachite green and crystal violet in fish.

Analytical Method ELISA.
06
