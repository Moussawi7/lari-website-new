KFARSHAKHNA STATION

Laboratory & Field Agricultural Activities

Kfarshakhna Station carries out laboratory analysis related to soil, water, plant tissue, olive fruits and olive oil.

The Station also performs field activities, including the production of certified citrus and olive propagation material.

Survey projects and meteorological station monitoring contribute to agricultural research and technical support.

The Station combines analytical laboratory work with practical agricultural field activities.
