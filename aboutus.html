Agricultural Research
Who We Are

Research for a stronger agricultural future

The Lebanese Agricultural Research Institute has at its disposal twelve experimental stations covering approximately 280 hectares of agricultural land.

These stations are located in agricultural areas where subtropical and temperate crops are produced. Research projects are conducted within these stations to address problems facing the agricultural sector in their respective areas.

Through scientific research, field activities and cooperation, LARI works to contribute to the development and progress of agriculture in Lebanon.

Research connects science with the real needs of agriculture and farming communities.

Working Together

Building Strong International Research Partnerships

LARI works with regional and international organizations to strengthen agricultural research, exchange scientific knowledge and develop sustainable solutions for the agricultural sector.

Through these partnerships, the institute expands its research capabilities, shares expertise and benefits from international experience in agriculture, technology, innovation and sustainable development.
