ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Potato Research & Agronomic Development

The Agronomy & Protected Crops Department manages several potato projects encouraging and applying good agricultural practices.

New potato varieties are planted every year at Abdeh Station and in farmers' fields.

Data and information are collected about the morphology and behavior of these varieties under Akkar climatic conditions.

One of the main objectives is to identify potato varieties that are suitable for export.
MAIN ACTIVITIES

Potato Research & Field Evaluation

The department combines agronomic practices, varietal testing and field observations.

01

Potato Projects

Implementation of several research projects focused on potato production.

02

Good Agricultural Practices

Encouraging and applying good agricultural practices in potato production.

03

New Potato Varieties

New potato varieties are introduced and planted each year for evaluation.

04

Abdeh Station Trials

Potato varieties are tested directly in experimental plots at Abdeh Station.

05

Farmers' Field Trials

Varieties are also tested under real farming conditions in farmers' fields.

06

Data Collection

Collection of information about plant morphology, development and performance.

VARIETY EVALUATION

Studying Potato Performance

Each new variety is evaluated according to its characteristics and response to local conditions.

Morphology

Observation and recording of morphological characteristics of potato varieties.

Plant Behavior

Evaluation of how different varieties behave during cultivation.

Akkar Climate

Varieties are studied under the climatic conditions of the Akkar region.

Field Performance

Trials compare performance at the station and in farmers' fields.

EXPORT OBJECTIVE

Selecting Suitable Export Varieties

A major objective of the trials is to identify varieties that perform well locally and have export potential.

01

Variety Selection

Comparing new varieties to identify the best-performing options for production.

02

Local Adaptation

Assessing whether varieties are well adapted to Akkar conditions.

03

Export Potential

Identifying varieties that may be suitable for export markets.

RESEARCH PROCESS

From Variety Introduction to Selection

Potato varieties pass through several stages of field evaluation.

Introduce

New potato varieties are introduced annually.

Plant

Varieties are planted at Abdeh Station and farmers' fields.

Observe

Morphology and behavior are monitored during cultivation.

Evaluate

Performance under Akkar climatic conditions is evaluated.

Select

Suitable varieties are identified for future production and export.
