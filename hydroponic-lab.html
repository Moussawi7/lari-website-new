ABOUT THE LABORATORY

Hydroponic Plant & Nutrient Analysis

The Hydroponic Laboratory at Fanar Station is designed to analyze nutrient solutions and plant material used in hydroponic production systems.

The laboratory will analyze chemical and physical characteristics of nutrient solutions, including ions, trace elements, pH, salinity and conductivity.

Plant development will also be evaluated through different growth, biomass and chlorophyll measurements.

The Hydroponic Laboratory is currently under construction.

NUTRIENT SOLUTION

Nutrient & Chemical Analysis

The nutrient solution will be analyzed through a range of chemical, ionic and water-quality parameters.

01

Trace Elements

Analysis of trace elements present in hydroponic nutrient solutions.

02

Cationic Ions

Measurement of cationic ions present in nutrient solutions.

03

Anionic Ions

Determination of anionic ions in hydroponic solutions.

04

C, H, N, O & S

Analysis of carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, oxygen and sulfur.

05

Total Dissolved Solids

Measurement of Total Dissolved Solids (TDS).

06

pH

Measurement of nutrient solution acidity and alkalinity.

07

Salinity

Monitoring the salinity of nutrient solutions.

08

Conductivity

Measurement of electrical conductivity in nutrient solutions.

09

BOD

Measurement of Biochemical Oxygen Demand.

10

COD

Measurement of Chemical Oxygen Demand.

Number of Leaves

Root Length

04

05

06
