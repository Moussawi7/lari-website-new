TAL AMARA STATION

Pomology & Viticulture

Improving fruit cultivation through modern orchard management, variety trials, biological control and sustainable pest management.

Fruit Crops Viticulture Biological Control Orchard Management
OUR MISSION

Improving Fruit Cultivation in Lebanon

Research is directed toward improving the fruit cultivation sector in Lebanon by introducing and testing the newest concepts and technologies used in modern orchard management.

The Biological Control Laboratory focuses on the production of natural enemies, especially Cryptolaemus montrouzieri, to control mealybugs on crops such as grapes, Annona trees, citrus and ornamental plants.

Supporting modern fruit production while reducing dependence on chemical pest treatments through biological control.
OUR ACTIVITIES

Pomology & Viticulture Activities

Research activities combine variety evaluation, germplasm conservation, biological control and pest forecasting.

01

Variety Trials & Demonstration Plots

Establishing variety trials and demonstration plots for the introduction and adoption of new and improved fruit varieties.

Fruit Crops Research includes improved varieties of stone fruits and pome fruits.
02

Table Grape Germplasm Collection

Collection of germplasm for domestic table grape varieties.

Genetic Resources Domestic grape varieties are collected to support conservation and future research.
03

Biological Control

Production of Cryptolaemus montrouzieri to fight mealybugs on several agricultural crops.

Target Crops Grapes, Annona trees, citrus and other affected crops.
04

Pest Forecast Service

Studying insect dynamics and life cycles in the Bekaa Valley to support pest forecasting.

Early Alerts Forecast information helps farmers respond at the right time and reduce unnecessary chemical treatments.
05

Cryptolaemus Research

Studying the production and breeding of the natural enemy Cryptolaemus montrouzieri.

Planococcus Control The natural enemy is used to fight Planococcus on grapes and Annona trees.
BIOLOGICAL CONTROL

Natural Pest Management

Biological control provides a more sustainable way to manage mealybugs while reducing chemical pesticide use.

Target Pest

Mealybugs and Planococcus affecting important fruit crops.

Natural Enemy

Cryptolaemus montrouzieri is produced for biological pest-control activities.

Sustainable Control

Biological control helps reduce dependence on chemical treatments.

Protected Crops

Grapes, Annona trees, citrus and ornamental plants can benefit from the program.

PEST FORECASTING

Monitoring Insect Development

The department studies insect dynamics and life cycles in the Bekaa Valley to support better pest management decisions.

01

Monitoring

Observing the development and activity of important insects.

02

Life Cycle Study

Studying insect life cycles and seasonal development.

03

Farmer Alerts

Using research results to provide timely pest alerts.

04

Reduced Chemical Use

Better timing can help minimize unnecessary chemical treatments.
