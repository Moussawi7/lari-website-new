ABOUT THE UNIT

Plant Genetic Resources Unit

The Plant Genetic Resources Unit was established in 2004 at the Lebanese Agricultural Research Institute in Tal Amara.

LARI is the focal point of the International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture.

The institute is therefore responsible for supporting implementation of the Treaty components in Lebanon.

The unit works to characterize, preserve and sustainably use Lebanon's agricultural genetic diversity.
MISSION

Conservation & Characterization

The unit combines morphological, agronomic and molecular approaches to study Lebanese plant diversity.

01

Morphological Characterization

Characterization of traditional varieties and their wild relatives to valorize and preserve national plant diversity.

02

Agronomical Traits

Study of important morphological and agronomic characteristics of Lebanese cultivars.

03

Molecular Characterization

Genetic diversity is studied using molecular markers such as SSR, RAPD and ISSR.

04

Inter & Intra Diversity

Evaluation of diversity both between varieties and within individual cultivars.

ITPGRFA

International Treaty Activities

The unit supports Lebanon's work under the International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture.

01

Draft PGRFA Law

A national working group established at LARI prepared a draft law for management of Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture in Lebanon.

Draft Law 18 articles Conservation Sustainable use Access to PGRFA Fair benefit sharing
02

AOAD Support

Development of the draft law was supported by the Arab Organization for Agricultural Development.

03

Genetic Resource Networks

Updating information in national, regional and international genetic-resource networks.

04

Information Exchange

Facilitating information exchange at national and international levels.
