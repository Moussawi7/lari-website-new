DEPARTMENT OF IRRIGATION & AGROMETEOROLOGY

Sustainable Water Management

The Department of Irrigation and AgroMeteorology works on improving irrigation-water management for sustainable agriculture in Lebanon.

Farmers consume more than 70% of the country's fresh water, making efficient irrigation management one of the department's key priorities.

DIAM also works in line with the Ministry of Agriculture strategy and contributes to the implementation of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

Improving irrigation efficiency, agricultural resilience and water productivity through applied research and climate information.
RESEARCH ACTIVITIES

Irrigation Research & Field Trials

DIAM carries out research projects, applied research, demonstration plots and field trials on crop water requirements.

01

Crop Water Requirements

Research on the water requirements of different agricultural crops.

Crops Studied Vegetables, cereals, legumes, fruit trees and ornamental crops.
02

Soil Moisture Measurement

Crop water requirements are evaluated using soil-moisture measurements.

Water Monitoring Soil moisture data helps determine irrigation needs more accurately.
03

Lysimeter Research

DIAM uses weighing and drainage lysimeters in crop-water studies.

Measurement Tools Weighing lysimeters and drainage lysimeters support accurate irrigation research.
04

Evapotranspiration

Crop water requirements are also based on measured reference evapotranspiration.

ETo Reference evapotranspiration is used as an important indicator for irrigation scheduling.
WATER EFFICIENCY

More Crop Per Drop

Water Use Efficiency and Water Productivity are among the department's priority research axes.

Water Use Efficiency

Improving how efficiently irrigation water is used.

Water Productivity

Increasing agricultural output per unit of water used.

More Crop Per Drop

Introducing farmers to the concept of producing more crop with less water.

Water Harvesting

Introducing new techniques for harvesting and conserving water.

SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE

Water Management & Environmental Awareness

DIAM conducts multidisciplinary research addressing water scarcity and sustainable irrigated agriculture.

01

Environmental Awareness

Increasing environmental awareness related to water scarcity.

Sustainability Promoting long-term perspectives for sustainable irrigated agriculture.
02

Water Management Problems

Studying current problems related to water management in semi-arid ecosystems.

Solutions Research aims to identify suitable solutions for correcting water-management problems.
03

Irrigation Information

Providing sound irrigation information to water managers and users.

Users Information supports farmers, water managers and irrigation users.
04

Water Quality

DIAM also works on improving water quality for agricultural use.

Improved Water Resources Better water quality supports sustainable agricultural production.
WEATHER NETWORK

Weather Stations Across Lebanon

DIAM works with other LARI stations to maintain a large national network of weather stations.

60 Weather Stations

A large weather-station network is maintained across Lebanon.

Nationwide Coverage

Weather stations cover nearly every microclimatic zone of Lebanon.

Climate Data

Climatic data is gathered, collected and stored.

Data Accuracy

Collected climatic data is checked for accuracy.

AGROMETEOROLOGY SERVICES

Climate Information for Farmers

AgroMeteorology is an integral part of DIAM's responsibilities.

01

Agricultural Weather Forecast

Weather forecasts are provided in direct relation to agriculture.

02

Early Warning System

Early warnings for pests and diseases are provided in collaboration with other LARI departments.

03

Drought Monitoring

Drought events are monitored and communicated to support farmers.

04

Irrigation Advice

Irrigation recommendations are communicated to Lebanese farmers.

LARI-LEB APP

Agricultural Information on Mobile

Weather Forecast

Early Alerts

Irrigation Advice

Free Service
