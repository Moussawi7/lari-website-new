ABOUT THE UNIT

Pest Surveillance & Management

The Entomology Unit is specialized in pest surveillance and management in the agricultural and forest sectors.

The Unit has extensive experience in the management of fruit flies on olive and fruit trees, Lobesia botrana on grape, Tuta absoluta on tomato, Gypsy Moth on oak trees and Pine Shoot Beetle on pine trees.

The Unit also focuses on conservation of natural resources such as beneficial insects and insect-plant associations.

Farmers receive technical support and extension through seminars, conferences, exhibitions, booklets, media and the LARI-LEB forecast application.

The Unit works with all LARI stations to cover agricultural areas throughout Lebanon.

ONGOING PROJECTS

Current Pest Research Programs

Current projects address pest surveillance, integrated pest management and climate-smart agriculture.

01

CURE-XF

Monitoring vectors transmitting decline disease caused by Xylella fastidiosa in Lebanon.

Project Capacity Building and Raising Awareness in Europe and Third Countries to Cope with Xylella fastidiosa. Funding H2020 - Marie Curie.
02

Cherries & Table Grapes

Monitoring and management of pests on cherries and table grapes in Central and West Bekaa.

Program Private Sector Development Program (PSDP). Support Funded by the European Union and implemented by Expertise France.
03

AgriCAL

Pest forecasting and Integrated Pest Management under climate-smart agriculture.

Project Focus Climate Smart Agriculture Adaptative Capacity of Rural Communities in Lebanon.
TECHNICAL EXPERTISE
