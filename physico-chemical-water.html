ABOUT THE LABORATORY

Water Quality Analysis & Research

The Physico-Chemical Water Laboratory receives ice samples and different types of water samples, including drinking water, bottled water, irrigation water, well water, swimming-pool water, wastewater and slaughterhouse water.

The laboratory provides services to private and official institutions, ministries, municipalities and citizens from different regions of Lebanon.

In addition to routine laboratory testing, the laboratory carries out research focused on water quality in Lebanon and other projects related to water.

Supporting reliable water-quality assessment for citizens, institutions, agriculture and environmental management.

REPORTING STANDARDS

Results According to Sample Type

Laboratory results are issued according to the relevant national or international standards.

LIBNOR

Lebanese standards are used for applicable water samples.

FAO

Irrigation-water results are issued according to FAO requirements.

Ministry of Environment

Wastewater samples are evaluated according to Ministry of Environment requirements.

ANALYSIS TIME

Three to Seven Days

The average waiting time between receiving a sample and issuing the final certificate or report is approximately three to seven days.

01

Sample Receipt

Samples are received and registered for analysis.

02

Laboratory Analysis

Required physico-chemical parameters are tested.

03

Report Issued

Results are certified and issued to the client.
