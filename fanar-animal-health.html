ABOUT THE LABORATORY

National Reference Laboratory for Animal Diseases

Animal Health Laboratory (AHL) - Fanar is the national reference laboratory for animal diseases in Lebanon.

Laboratory personnel are prepared seven days a week to receive samples from unexpected situations and outbreaks of infectious animal diseases.

More than twenty-seven diseases affecting ruminants, poultry, horses and pets are diagnosed using serological and molecular tools.

Research focuses on pathogens and diseases of importance for animal health and public health, including zoonotic diseases.

AHL-Fanar is the only laboratory in Lebanon with the capacity to diagnose Rabies.
