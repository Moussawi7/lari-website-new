ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Research Supporting Rural Communities

Abdeh Station staff use their theoretical knowledge and scientific experience to support rural development activities.

Their extensive field experience helps connect agricultural research with the practical needs of farmers and rural communities.

Results obtained through applied research are used to help build rural development projects and improve agricultural practices.

The department combines research, practical field experience and farmer training to support rural development.
MAIN ACTIVITIES

Rural Development & Farmer Support

Staff experience and research are transformed into practical agricultural development activities.

01

Theoretical Expertise

Scientific and technical knowledge is used to support rural development planning.

02

Field Experience

Practical agricultural field experience contributes to the design of development projects.

03

Applied Research

Results from applied agricultural research are used in rural-development work.

04

Development Projects

Staff contribute to building rural development projects based on research and field needs.

05

Farmer Training

Participation in training activities designed to transfer practical agricultural knowledge to farmers.
