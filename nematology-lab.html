ABOUT THE LABORATORY

Plant-Parasitic Nematode Research

Plant-parasitic nematodes cause more than $100 billion in annual losses to world agriculture.

Cyst nematodes, including Heterodera and Globodera species, together with root-knot nematodes of the genus Meloidogyne, contribute to more than 80% of these losses.

The Plant Nematology Laboratory at LARI provides technical support through the detection of plant-parasitic nematodes using different extraction techniques and morphological identification.

Supporting plant health through nematode detection, identification, research and sustainable management.
RESEARCH AREAS

Nematodes Studied by the Laboratory

Fundamental research covers several economically important groups of plant-parasitic nematodes.

01

Root-Knot Nematodes

Research on root-knot nematodes belonging to the genus Meloidogyne.

Research Focus Detection, identification and management of root-knot nematode populations.
02

Cyst Nematodes

Research on cyst nematodes, including Heterodera and Globodera species.

Potato Health Includes yearly surveys of potato cyst nematodes.
03

Foliar Nematodes

Research on foliar nematodes, particularly those affecting pine trees.

04

Stem & Bulb Nematodes

Research and identification of nematodes affecting stems and bulbs.

05

Citrus Nematodes

Research on nematode genera associated with citrus crops.

06

Fruit Tree Nematodes

Identification and research on nematodes associated with fruit trees.

07

Olive Nematodes

Research on nematodes affecting olive trees and their production.

08

Banana Nematodes

Research and identification of nematodes associated with banana crops.

09

Grapevine Nematodes

Research on plant-parasitic nematodes related to grape production.

LABORATORY WORK

Detection & Identification

The laboratory uses specialized methods to detect and identify plant-parasitic nematodes.

Extraction

Different extraction techniques are used according to sample type and nematode group.

Morphological Identification

Nematodes are identified using their morphological characteristics.

Sample Testing

Soil, plant material and peat samples can be analyzed.

Technical Support

Laboratory results support agricultural management and plant-health decisions.

SUSTAINABLE MANAGEMENT

Bio-Fumigation Research

The laboratory studies sustainable alternatives for managing nematode populations.

01

Bio-Fumigation

Bio-fumigation approaches are investigated as a way to reduce nematode populations.

02

Economic Threshold

Management strategies aim to reduce nematode populations below economically damaging levels.

03

Environmental Protection

Sustainable approaches can help reduce dependence on environmentally damaging chemical nematicides.

NATIONAL SUPPORT

Supporting Plant Health Regulations

The Unit supports the Lebanese Ministry of Agriculture in implementing plant-health regulations.

Ministry of Agriculture

Supporting the implementation of national plant-health regulations.

Potato Surveys

Annual surveys for potato cyst nematodes have been conducted since 2012.

Potato Export

Laboratory testing helps meet requirements for potato export.

EU Requirements

Surveys and testing support compliance with requirements for export to the European Union.
