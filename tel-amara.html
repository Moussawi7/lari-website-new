TAL AMARA STATION

Agricultural Research & Innovation

Tal Amara is one of the research stations of the Lebanese Agricultural Research Institute (LARI).

The station brings together specialized laboratories, research units and agricultural activities dedicated to supporting sustainable agriculture and scientific research in Lebanon.

Select any research unit or laboratory below to discover its activities, research areas, services and staff.

Nineteen specialized research units and laboratories are available to explore at Tal Amara Station.
