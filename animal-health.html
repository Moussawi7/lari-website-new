ANIMAL HEALTH DEPARTMENT

Protecting Animal & Public Health

The Animal Health Department was established at Tel Al-Amara Station in 2012.

Its main activities include analyzing and inspecting samples received from quarantine, veterinary clinics and farms in the Bekaa region.

The department's staff also coordinates and cooperates with international laboratories and organizations to maintain a permanent update of information related to animal health.

Combining field surveillance, laboratory diagnostics and international cooperation to improve animal health and livestock productivity.
OUR OBJECTIVES

Animal Health Objectives

The department works to protect animals, people and livestock production through surveillance, testing and technical development.

Protect Animal Health

Protect animal health through surveillance, field inspections and laboratory testing.

Protect Public Health

Protect public and human health from diseases shared between animals and humans, including zoonotic diseases.

Improve Productivity

Protect livestock and improve their productivity through technical and scientific support.

Develop Technical Capacity

Develop technical capacities in animal-health testing, diagnosis and disease monitoring.

OUR ACTIVITIES

Animal Health Activities

These activities aim to protect ruminants and poultry from diseases that affect productivity and marketing capacity.

01

Poultry Farm Health Review

Reviewing the general health status of broiler and laying poultry farms.

Health Monitoring Farm conditions and the general health status of poultry are monitored and evaluated.
02

Poultry Disease Testing

Laboratory testing of major poultry diseases using ELISA techniques.

Diseases Tested Newcastle disease, infectious bronchitis and Gumboro disease.
03

Field Disease Surveys

Conducting field surveys of diseases in sheep, goats and poultry farms.

Surveyed Diseases Johne's disease and Brucella in ruminants, and Newcastle disease in poultry.
04

Poultry Research

Implementation of research work in the poultry farm at Terbol Station.

Research Location Poultry research activities are conducted at LARI's Terbol Station.
05

Student Training

Receiving students from agricultural technical colleges and institutes.

Practical Education Students gain exposure to laboratory testing, animal-health surveillance and technical agricultural work.
06

International Cooperation

Coordinating with international laboratories and organizations working in animal health.

Continuous Updates Cooperation supports the permanent updating of scientific and technical information related to animal health.
