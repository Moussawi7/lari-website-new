Since World War II
Our Beginning

The beginning of agricultural research in Lebanon

Government research aimed at improving agricultural production in Lebanon dates back to the establishment of Tal Amara Station in the Bekaa Valley immediately after World War II.

Tal Amara was initially established as an agricultural training center and later became an important foundation for organized agricultural scientific research in Lebanon.

The creation of Tal Amara Station marked an important step toward organized agricultural research in Lebanon.

LARI Through Time

Our Historical Journey

Follow the important milestones that transformed agricultural research in Lebanon and led to the creation of the Lebanese Agricultural Research Institute.

World War II
01

Establishment of Tal Amara Station

Government research for improving agricultural production in Lebanon began with the establishment of Tal Amara Station in the Bekaa Valley immediately after World War II.

The station initially operated as an agricultural training center.

1957
02

Department of Agricultural Scientific Research

In 1957, Tal Amara became part of the Department of Agricultural Scientific Research of the Ministry of Agriculture.

This represented an important development in organizing agricultural research activities under the Ministry.

1960s
03

Expansion of Research Stations

Additional agricultural research stations were established in different regions of Lebanon.

Tourbol – Bekaa Animal production
Abde – North Citrus research
Sour – South Citrus research
1964
04

Autonomous Public Institution

In 1964, the Department of Agricultural Scientific Research was reorganized as an autonomous public institution.

It became the Lebanese Agricultural Research Institute (LARI) working under the supervision of the Minister of Agriculture.

1970
05

Expansion of Agricultural Research

During the 1970s, LARI continued to expand its agricultural research activities and strengthen its network of research stations across Lebanon.

By the end of the decade, cooperation with international agricultural organizations also increased, supporting the development of scientific research and agricultural expertise in Lebanon.

Now
06

12 Research Stations Across Lebanon

LARI operates 12 research stations across Lebanon, providing agricultural research, scientific services, innovation, and support to farmers in different regions of the country.

The 12 research stations are Tal Amara, Lebaa, Tyre, Fanar, Kfarshakhna, Abdeh, Hasbaya, Keserwan, Baakline, Terbol, Hermel, and Kfardan.

Our Legacy

From one station to a national research institution

LARI's history reflects the gradual development of agricultural scientific research in Lebanon.

Beginning with Tal Amara Station after World War II, agricultural research expanded through new stations, specialized research fields and cooperation with international agricultural organizations.

The establishment of LARI as an autonomous public institution in 1964 created a stronger framework for agricultural research under the supervision of the Minister of Agriculture.
