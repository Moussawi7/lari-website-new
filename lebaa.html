LARI RESEARCH STATION

Lebaa Station

Agricultural research in fruit production, plant protection, biodiversity, irrigation, biological control and sustainable farming.

Fruit Trees Plant Protection Irrigation Biodiversity
OUR MISSION

Supporting Agriculture in South Lebanon

Lebaa Station is located in the Jizzin region of South Lebanon at an altitude of approximately 350 meters above sea level.

The station belongs to the Litani River Authority and occupies an area of approximately 6 hectares.

Annual mean precipitation is approximately 850 mm.

More than eight species of fruit trees are cultivated at Lebaa Station, including olive, apple, peach, pomegranate, avocado, citrus, nuts and apricot.

Vegetables are cultivated in both open fields and greenhouses.

Research at Lebaa Station aims to help farmers increase agricultural production and improve product quality through plant protection, biodiversity and irrigation research.

RESEARCH STUDIES

Main Research Areas

Research at Lebaa focuses on pest management, irrigation and fertilization, new crops, genetic-resource conservation and domestication of wild plants.

01

Integrated Pest Management

Researching sustainable methods to manage agricultural pests and reduce crop losses.

Research Topics Management of Tuta absoluta under greenhouse conditions using biopesticides. Status, distribution and parasitism rate of olive fruit-fly parasitoids in Lebanon. Surveillance and management of fruit flies in Lebanon. Pest and disease forecast program and early-warning services.
02

Irrigation & Fertilization Strategies

Evaluating irrigation and fertilization strategies for improved crop production and product quality.

Research Topics Influence of irrigation and harvesting time on olive-oil composition. Effect of organic mulch and irrigation frequency on Origanum syriacum yield. Effect of nutrient fertilization time on saffron yield in Baaklin. Management of iron deficiency in peach trees under calcareous soils.
03

New Varieties & Species

Introducing promising new crops and studying their adaptation to Lebanese agricultural conditions.

Crops Studied Quinoa and saffron. Saffron cultivation in Lebanon. Effect of corm density on saffron yield. Effect of soil type on saffron yield.
04

Genetic Resources

Conserving agricultural biodiversity and establishing germplasm collections for local and imported species.

Conservation Projects Conservation and characterization of local olive varieties in Lebanon. Research on carob trees as an important Lebanese patrimony.
05

Origanum syriacum Domestication

Researching the domestication, conservation and propagation of Origanum syriacum.

Current Research Germplasm of different Origanum syriacum ecotypes. Morphological and genetic analysis of Origanum accessions from Lebaa and Hasbaya stations. Vegetative propagation using stem cuttings and different hormone doses under greenhouse conditions.
