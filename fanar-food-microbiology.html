ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Food Safety & Microbiological Control

The Food Microbiology Department (FMD) is part of Fanar Station Laboratory at LARI.

Its main aim is food microbiology control on imported, exported and locally produced food items.

The department has been accredited by ESYD since 2012 for eleven ISO methods.

The department adopts fast automated systems such as TEMPO® for enumeration of quality indicators and VIDAS® for pathogen detection.

These automated systems allow the department to provide results from approximately 24 hours up to four days maximum.

Accredited microbiological testing combined with modern automated systems helps support faster and reliable food-safety analysis.
