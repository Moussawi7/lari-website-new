FANAR STATION

Antibiotics & Pesticides Residues

Detection and analysis of antibiotic, hormone and pesticide residues in food, water, soil and agricultural products.

Antibiotics Pesticides Residue Analysis Research & Training
LABORATORY ACTIVITIES

Residue Detection & Food Safety

The laboratory determines antibiotic and hormone residues in meat, fish, dairy products, honey and other products.

Pesticide residues are determined in vegetables, fruits, pickles, cereals, tea, water, soil and other agricultural samples.

The laboratory also performs research projects in collaboration with universities and receives Master's and PhD students for research and practical training.

Supporting food safety, agricultural control and scientific research through specialized residue analysis.
MAIN ACTIVITIES

Laboratory Services

The department carries out routine analysis, scientific research and student training.

01

Antibiotic Residues

Determination of antibiotic residues in meat, fish, dairy products, honey and other food products.

02

Hormone Residues

Analysis of hormone residues in selected animal-based food products.

03

Pesticide Residues

Determination of pesticide residues in vegetables, fruits, cereals, tea and pickles.

04

Water Analysis

Detection of pesticide residues in water samples.

05

Soil Analysis

Testing soil samples for agricultural pesticide residues.

06

Student Training

Receiving and training Master's and PhD students.

ROUTINE WORK

Sample Sources

Samples analyzed by the laboratory are received mainly from public institutions and private companies.

Private Companies

Samples are submitted by private companies for routine analysis.

Ministry of Agriculture

Samples are received from the Lebanese Ministry of Agriculture.

Ministry of Economy & Trade

Samples are also received from the Lebanese Ministry of Economy and Trade.

Local Market

Testing supports products intended for local consumption.

Export Market

Analysis also supports products intended for export.

RESEARCH PROJECTS

Collaborative Research

Research projects focus on pesticide and antibiotic residues in food and biological samples.

01

Pesticides in Market Foods

Determination of pesticide residues in avocado, ready-to-eat food for children and frozen vegetables from several international brands.

Analytical Protocol European Protocol SANCO/12571/2017. Collaboration Université de Pau et des Pays de l'Adour, France.
02

Organic Products

Determination of pesticide residues in organic products collected from the local market.

Protocol SANCO/12571/2017. Collaboration Faculty of Public Health - Lebanese University.
03

Residues in Breast Milk

Determination of agricultural pesticide and antibiotic residues in breast milk of Syrian refugee lactating mothers.

Collaboration Beirut Arab University.
04

Antibiotics in Milk & Dairy

Determination of antibiotic residues in different samples of milk and dairy products.

Funding Lebanese National Council for Scientific Research. Partners LARI, Lebanese University and University of Balamand.
