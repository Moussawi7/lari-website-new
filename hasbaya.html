ABOUT HASBAYA STATION

Laboratory Services & Applied Research

The laboratories of Hasbaya Station have a double role: routine analysis of water, soil and olive oil samples, and participation in research projects.

These research projects support the agricultural sector and the environment in Lebanon.

Hasbaya Station combines routine laboratory work, farmer services and scientific research in agriculture and environmental protection.

RESEARCH PROJECTS

Current Research & Development Projects

Hasbaya Station works on projects related to olive genetic resources, rural resilience and water quality.

01

Centennial Olive Trees in Lebanon

Study of geographical, biological and agricultural characteristics of ancient olive trees.

Collaboration Hasbaya Station Olive & Olive Oil Department, Tal Amara UMR AGAP, Montpellier, France Research Areas Molecular and morphological characterization Fatty acids, phenolic compounds and oil content Phenological performance under contrasting agroclimatic conditions Climate-change projections
02

OLIVE – Place-of-Origin Quality Brand

Supporting olive growers and cooperatives in Hasbaya through improved resilience and olive-sector development.

Collaboration Hasbaya Station Centro Laici Italiani per le Missioni - CELIM El Khalil Foundation Target Improve resilience conditions of at least 2,935 Lebanese in rural areas of Hasbaya District involved in the olive-oil sector.
03

Surface & Groundwater Quality

Research highlighting water-pollution problems in Hasbaya and Marjaayoun.

Approach Comprehensive sampling Routine parameter analysis Heavy-metal analysis
