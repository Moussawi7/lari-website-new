OLIVE & OLIVE OIL DEPARTMENT

Developing Lebanon's Olive Sector

The Olive and Olive Oil Department at Tal Amara Station was established in 2011.

The department aims to develop the olive and olive oil sector in Lebanon through research, quality improvement, genetic-resource conservation and technical support.

Supporting better olive production, higher-quality olive oil and the conservation of Lebanon's valuable olive genetic resources.
OUR OBJECTIVES

Department Objectives

The department combines scientific research, quality control, conservation and advisory services to strengthen Lebanon's olive sector.

01

Olive Variety Characterization

Characterizing Lebanese olive varieties and studying the characteristics of oils extracted from them.

Research Focus Morphological, agronomical and oil-quality characterization of local olive varieties.
02

Improve Productivity

Improving the productivity and agricultural performance of olive trees.

Olive Production Supporting improved cultivation practices and better-performing olive orchards.
03

Olive Oil Quality

Improving the quality of olive oil produced in Lebanon.

Quality Control Supporting analytical testing and improved production practices.
04

Prevent Olive Oil Adulteration

Providing scientific assistance against adulteration of olive oil in Lebanon.

Authenticity Analytical methods help evaluate olive-oil composition and authenticity.
05

Lebanese Standards

Contributing to the development of Lebanese standards for olive oil and table olives.

Standardization Supporting quality criteria and standards for Lebanese olive products.
06

Geographical Origins

Supporting added value for local olive oil through development of protected geographical origins.

Local Identity Helping strengthen the identity and market value of Lebanese olive oil.
07

Genetic Resource Protection

Cataloguing and protecting olive genetic resources and centennial olive trees in Lebanon.

Conservation Preserving valuable Lebanese olive germplasm and ancient trees.
08

Farmer Advisory Services

Providing extension and advisory services to olive farmers.

Farmer Support Sharing scientific and practical guidance with producers.
09

University Training

Training students from different Lebanese universities.

Education Providing practical exposure to olive research and oil analysis.
10

International Collaboration

Introducing new techniques, equipment and analytical methods through international projects.

Cooperation Collaboration with leading European laboratories specialized in olive genetic resources and olive-oil analysis.
LABORATORY ACTIVITIES

Research & Analytical Activities

The department performs olive characterization, oil extraction and detailed quality and compositional analyses.

Olive Characterization

Morphological and agronomical characterization of local and widespread olive varieties.

Abencor Extraction

Olive oil extraction using the Abencor laboratory system.

Oil Content

Analysis of oil content in olive paste, pomace and olive mill wastewater.

Quality Analysis

Free acidity, peroxide value, UV absorbance and oxidative stability analysis.

OLIVE OIL ANALYSIS

Quality & Composition Testing

Modern analytical methods are used to characterize olive-oil quality, stability and composition.

01

Free Acidity

Determination of free acidity as an important olive-oil quality parameter.

02

Peroxide Value

Measurement of oxidation-related changes in olive oil.

03

UV Absorbance

Measurement of K232, K270 and delta-K values.

04

Oxidative Stability

Determination of oxidative stability index using the Rancimat method.

05

Fatty Acid Composition

Olive-oil fatty acid composition analyzed using GC-FID.

06

Phenolic Profile

Analysis of phenolic compounds using HPLC-DAD.
