MYCOTOXINS LABORATORY

Protecting Food & Public Health

The Mycotoxins Laboratory was established at the Lebanese Agricultural Research Institute in Tal Amara Station in 2010.

The laboratory aims to highlight the importance of subjecting food and feedstuffs exposed to mycotoxin contamination risks to laboratory analysis in order to protect consumers and maintain public health.

Laboratory monitoring helps identify mycotoxin contamination in food and feed before it becomes a risk to consumers.
LABORATORY TESTING

Mycotoxins Tested

The laboratory tests food and feed samples for several important mycotoxins.

01

Aflatoxins & Aflatoxin B1

Testing for aflatoxins including Aflatoxin B1 in food and feed samples.

Contamination Monitoring Samples exposed to possible aflatoxin contamination are analyzed in the laboratory.
02

Aflatoxin M1

Detection and determination of Aflatoxin M1 levels.

Laboratory Analysis Testing supports food-safety and contamination monitoring.
03

Ochratoxin A

Analysis of Ochratoxin A contamination in food and feed.

Food Safety Laboratory testing helps evaluate possible contamination risks.
04

Deoxynivalenol

Detection of Deoxynivalenol in food and feed products.

Sample Testing Food and feed samples are analyzed for contamination levels.
05

Zearalenone

Laboratory testing for Zearalenone contamination.

Quality Monitoring Testing supports the control of contaminated food and feed materials.
ANALYTICAL METHODS

Methods of Analysis

The laboratory determines mycotoxin levels in food and feed using analytical laboratory methods.

ELISA

Current determination of mycotoxin levels is performed using the ELISA method.

HPLC

HPLC analysis is planned as an additional method for mycotoxin determination.

Food Samples

Food products exposed to contamination risks are analyzed.

Feed Samples

Animal feed is also monitored for possible mycotoxin contamination.

MARKET MONITORING

Food & Feed Sample Monitoring

Laboratory staff regularly collect food and feed samples from markets for mycotoxin analysis.

01

Market Sample Collection

Food and feed samples are collected regularly from markets for laboratory testing.

Monitoring Samples are evaluated to determine whether mycotoxin contamination is present.
02

Wheat Flour Analysis

Recent laboratory activities include aflatoxin analysis in wheat flour samples.

Aflatoxin Testing Wheat flour samples are examined for potential aflatoxin contamination.
03

Thyme Mix Analysis

Thyme mix samples are also analyzed for possible aflatoxin contamination.

Food Safety Testing Analysis supports monitoring of food products available on the market.
QUALITY ASSURANCE

APTECA Proficiency Testing

The laboratory participates annually in external proficiency testing to assess analytical performance.

Yearly Participation

The laboratory participates every year in the APTECA proficiency testing program.

Two Rounds

Two proficiency-testing rounds are completed each year.

Satisfactory Results

The laboratory achieves satisfactory results in the proficiency tests.

Reliable Analysis

Proficiency testing helps support reliable laboratory performance.
