TAL AMARA STATION

Chemical Water Laboratory

Chemical analysis of potable, irrigation and wastewater to support water-quality evaluation and reliable laboratory monitoring.

Potable Water Irrigation Water Wastewater Chemical Analysis
CHEMICAL WATER LABORATORY

Water Quality Analysis

The role of the Chemical Water Laboratory is to perform analysis of potable water, irrigation water and wastewater.

Laboratory results are evaluated according to LIBNOR specifications.

Supporting reliable water-quality assessment through chemical analysis and standardized laboratory testing.
WATER TYPES

Water Analyzed

The laboratory performs chemical testing for three main categories of water.

Potable Water

Chemical analysis of water intended for drinking.

Irrigation Water

Chemical evaluation of water used for agricultural irrigation.

Wastewater

Chemical analysis of wastewater using specific quality parameters.

LIBNOR Specifications

Laboratory results are based on LIBNOR specifications.

POTABLE & IRRIGATION WATER

Elements Tested

Potable and irrigation water samples are analyzed for important chemical and mineral parameters.

01

pH

Measurement of water acidity and alkalinity.

02

Conductivity

Measurement of the electrical conductivity of water.

03

Sodium

Determination of sodium concentration in water.

04

Potassium

Determination of potassium levels in water samples.

05

Calcium

Analysis of calcium concentration in water.

06

Magnesium

Measurement of magnesium content in water samples.

07

Chloride

Determination of chloride levels in water.

08

Bicarbonate

Measurement of bicarbonate concentration in water.

09

Sulfate

Analysis of sulfate levels in water samples.

10

Nitrate

Determination of nitrate concentration in water.

11

Heavy Metals

Analysis of heavy metals present in water samples.

WASTEWATER ANALYSIS

Wastewater Parameters

Wastewater is tested using several chemical and physical indicators of water quality.

BOD

Measurement of Biochemical Oxygen Demand.

COD

Measurement of Chemical Oxygen Demand.

Total Phosphorus

Determination of total phosphorus levels.

Total Nitrogen

Measurement of total nitrogen concentration.

Total Suspended Solids

Determination of suspended solids in wastewater.

pH

Measurement of wastewater acidity and alkalinity.

LABORATORY IMPACT

Supporting Better Water Management

Chemical water analysis supports drinking-water quality, agricultural irrigation and wastewater monitoring.

01

Potable Water Quality

Supporting evaluation of the chemical quality of drinking water.

02

Agricultural Water

Evaluating irrigation-water chemistry for agricultural use.

03

Wastewater Monitoring

Measuring important wastewater quality indicators.

04

Standardized Results

Laboratory results follow LIBNOR specifications.
